## What it is

DAG execution, workflow engines, event-driven execution, queues, schedulers, retries, timeouts, distributed and durable execution, job management, state persistence.

## Why it exists

Multi-step work needs delivery guarantees: retries, timeouts, durable state, and recovery. This is where AI workloads meet classical distributed-systems problems.
